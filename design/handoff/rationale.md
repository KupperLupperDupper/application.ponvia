# Ponvia — palette & mood rationale

The palette starts from a single deep sea-green, `#1F6A5C`: dark enough to hold AA contrast against white as both text and fill, desaturated enough that it never reads as a health-app "success green" cheering you on. Everything else is a warm-leaning neutral built around it — a slightly green-tinted off-white (`#F6F8F7`) in light, a near-black with the same tint (`#0E1412`) in dark — so the interface feels like paper and ink rather than glass and chrome. The one deliberate second hue is a muted ochre (`#8A6238` / `#E0BC88`) used only for an *upward* delta; paired with an arrow icon and the word "Up", it distinguishes direction without ever implying failure, and the downward delta borrows the brand green for the same reason — it is the app's neutral, not its reward. Weight is a number people can feel judged by, so the design spends its contrast on the number itself and keeps the accent for one thing at a time: the primary action, the highlighted goal, the trend line. Generous whitespace, 24–28dp radii and short, standard-eased motion do the rest of the emotional work — the app should feel like it opens, tells you the truth, and gets out of the way.
